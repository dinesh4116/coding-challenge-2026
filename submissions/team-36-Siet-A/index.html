Why "conversions > 0" is not enough

A term with 2 clicks and 1 conversion shows a 50% conversion rate, but two clicks cannot tell luck from a real winner. This tool measures how sure it can be.

Promote needs enough clicks and conversions, and the 95% lower bound (Wilson) of the term's conversion rate must beat the account average.

Block needs a low chance of seeing this few conversions at the account's normal rate, plus meaningful spend.

Needs review covers everything with too little data, and promising terms without enough proof. Groups and your own corrections can turn these into suggestions.
